Rule 14 of 38 · Enabled

Based colour abutting negative space must be trapped

TRAP_AT_NEGATIVE_SPACE

Rules view fields

Status

trap_required_below_pct: 70

Remediation
A colour on the underbase meets bare garment at its edge with no trap. Ask seps.io for the standard 0.75pt trap. Shapes fully enclosed by a knocked-out colour do not need one - a minimal choke there is optional habit, not a requirement.

Full stored rule details

Description
A based colour needs a trap only where its perimeter meets negative space (bare garment). Where the colour is bounded by another ink - typically a white or a colour that is itself knocked out of the underbase - it sits snugly inside that ink and needs no choke. Measured as the fraction of each colorant's perimeter adjacent to another ink. White is exempt: it is the bounding ink and needs no choke over base.
