description: Approved sales follow-up direction, seasonal events and custom client/contact fields; future email boundaries.
---

The user wants sales-management assistance identifying clients who ordered in a
past period, including this time last year, upcoming order anniversaries, and
selected months last year, with views by invoice owner. The user approved building
the follow-up system, calendar/list/board views, niche/client seasonal events, and
custom client/contact fields.

**Why:** The user wants proactive client check-ins based on prior ordering patterns.

**How to apply:** Use a customer-grouped follow-up view alongside Pipeline, with prior
invoice evidence, owner and niche filters, and explicit date semantics. Do not treat an invoice
owner as permanent customer ownership or an anniversary as a confirmed reorder.

Niche events appear on matching customer records, but do not automatically create
hundreds of tasks. Staff select which customers need outreach; specific client
occurrences can be excluded or moved. Personal details such as birthdays and
shirt sizes belong to individual contacts when appropriate, not a whole company.
Annual dates need no birth year and remain internal.

**Why:** The approved design distinguishes general seasonal opportunities from
actual staff commitments and keeps personal information attributable to the right person.

The user explicitly wants future email integration for manual use, AI drafts,
automated follow-up, and check-in schedules. This is not authorization to send
messages now. Keep source event/invoice/contact identity and edit history stable
for that integration; staff marking “Contacted” is not proof of email delivery.

**Why:** Future communication needs to connect to the same follow-up records,
without treating a status change as an external communication.

**How to apply:** Add email only through explicit permission and existing mailbox
access controls. Automated sending needs separate opt-in, recipient/consent rules,
reply-aware cancellation and delivery audit; never infer sending consent from a
calendar event, niche assignment, custom date field, or manual follow-up status.
