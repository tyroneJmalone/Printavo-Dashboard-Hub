name: Calculator multi-style quoting direction
description: User's shared-artwork quoting workflow and proposed simplification of Printavo targeting.
---

The user often quotes more than one garment style with the same artwork/design printed or embroidered on them.

**Why:** They want to apply the same pricing matrix across several styles and push those styles as separate Printavo line items within one line-item group.

**How to apply:** Keep garment costs distinct while sharing decoration settings. The user also wants the invoice/quote selected under Push to Printavo to be the sole destination, without a separate required quote-number entry above. These are discussion-stage directions, not authorization to implement. Whether quantity breaks apply independently to alternatives or to combined quantities of a mixed order remains unresolved.
