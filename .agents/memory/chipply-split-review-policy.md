name: Chipply split review boundaries
description: Conservative interpretation of intentional invoice splits versus duplicates.
---

Combining invoices is an explicit Hub review, not inferred linking or permission to relax garment identity. Keep ordinary single-invoice diagnostics separate. Do not allocate one source process across conflicting production methods.

**Why:** Equal totals cannot establish whether invoices represent an intentional split, duplicates, or different garments. The requested extension requires review before attribution and must never write Printavo or duplicate Chipply dollars.

**How to apply:** Require exact combined style/color/size coverage and current evidence for all selected invoices. Conservatively block identical normalized invoice garment breakdowns even when two legitimate equal halves are possible; supporting those needs additional provenance, not a checkbox bypass. Changed evidence requires a fresh review.
