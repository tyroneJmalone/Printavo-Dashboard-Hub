---
name: KB import quality verification
description: Verify meaning on actual PDF text, not merely successful draft insertion.
---

Validate semantic quality against the actual extracted document before declaring a KB import repaired. A successful import and valid draft titles do not establish that rules, exceptions, and fees stayed together.

**Why:** A recovered PDF import technically succeeded while yielding dozens of single-word drafts. PDF text-layer whitespace can separate words within sentences; minimum-length filtering can also silently remove short but important rules.

**How to apply:** Use a non-persisting AI preview on representative extracted text and inspect the resulting bodies. Check short positive and negative rules, amounts, and exceptions. Keep staff-only source breadcrumbs outside AI-visible bodies during consolidation.
