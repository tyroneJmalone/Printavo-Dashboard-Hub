---
name: KB sharing policy
description: User-approved disclosure defaults and separation of usage groups from topic categories.
---

Treat Customer-Shareable, Internal Operational Knowledge, and Brand/Voice Guidance as usage boundaries, not merely topic labels. Existing knowledge remains internal until explicitly classified; do not infer disclosure permission from a topic such as pricing or shipping.

**Why:** The user explicitly approved conservative defaults on 2026-09-15. A pricing entry can contain internal costing, and approval alone is not permission to disclose it to customers.

**How to apply:** Category assignments set defaults for future entries/imports, never retroactively widen existing entries. Reclassification of approved entries needs re-approval. Brand guidance shapes wording only, not facts or policy promises. Editable rules must not override these hard boundaries.

The staff knowledge playground may explicitly include drafts, with draft/approved attribution, without changing live approval requirements or usage-group boundaries.

**Why:** The user wants to evaluate knowledge before approving it, rather than publishing untested content and removing it afterward.
