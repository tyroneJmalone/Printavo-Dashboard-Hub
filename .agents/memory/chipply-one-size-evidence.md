name: Chipply one-size reconciliation
description: Why Printavo Other needs narrowly scoped one-size interpretation rather than blanket size equivalence.
---

Printavo “Other” is not a general synonym for every Chipply size. Permit one-size interpretation only with unambiguous same-product/color source evidence, retaining exact quantity checks; never equate Other to ordinary apparel sizes.

**Why:** The user's correctly populated cap and duffel invoices used Other where Chipply used Adjustable or OSFA. Strict label comparison falsely blocked entire process groups, including matching apparel in the same group. Blindly equating Other with all source sizes would hide real discrepancies.

**How to apply:** Keep this exception conservative and explain it in verification evidence. Step 3 invoice verification and Step 5 complete-process quantity verification are separate checks; a group-level failure does not mean every displayed product is wrong.
