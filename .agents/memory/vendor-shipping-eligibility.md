---
name: Vendor shipping eligibility
description: Account-specific shipping confirmation and conservative queue estimates
---

Do not seed SanMar or S&S shipping thresholds from public promotions or assumptions. An account-specific confirmation source, confirmation date, and review deadline are required before terms can support estimates.

**Why:** No actual account terms were supplied or verified when shipping-progress support was introduced. Public vendor policies are not proof of the connected account's terms.

**How to apply:** Leave terms unset until an authorized person records account confirmation. Treat a queue selection as one proposed purchase only; never pool separate purchases or warehouse shipments to assert eligibility. Unknown method, warehouse, or free-text restrictions remain unresolved, and resolved costs still produce estimates rather than guarantees.
